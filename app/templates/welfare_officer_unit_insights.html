Privacy Controls

Why Some Unit Insights May Be Withheld

VeerPulse does not infer or fabricate aggregate contributors when the current database does not contain enough personnel.

Minimum Group Gate Enabled

Distribution percentages are withheld until at least five current personnel assessments are available.

Individual Ranking Disabled

The aggregate endpoint does not return a personnel ranking.

Unsupported Contributors Not Shown

Reduced-rest, duty, deployment or training percentages are not displayed unless supported by real aggregate data.

Welfare Planning

Suggested Unit-Level Planning Areas

These are planning prompts for authorized human review and are not automatic operational decisions.

Recovery Planning

Review areas where reduced rest patterns remain sustained and consider appropriate recovery opportunities where operationally possible.

Workload Balancing

Identify persistent high-load patterns and consider redistribution of non-critical workload subject to command requirements.

Deployment Planning

Use aggregated deployment-duration trends to support welfare-aware planning without exposing unnecessary individual details.

Training Scheduling

Review training periods that repeatedly overlap high-duty periods and consider timing adjustments where authorized.

Welfare Support Capacity

Use aggregate trends to plan counselling, welfare conversations and support availability.

Follow-Up Monitoring

Review whether unit-level patterns improve after authorized welfare interventions and recovery actions.

Privacy Safeguards

Aggregate Insights Without Unnecessary Exposure

Unit analytics should use aggregated, minimum-necessary information wherever possible.
Individual wellness details should remain restricted to authorized roles and legitimate welfare review.
Unit-level statistics must not be used to rank personnel or automatically generate disciplinary decisions.
Final welfare and operational decisions remain with authorized human officers or commanders.
Privacy-Protected Aggregate View. Unit-level percentages are produced only from stored current assessments after the minimum group-size rule is met. When the database contains too few personnel, VeerPulse shows “Insufficient Aggregate Data” instead of displaying sample or fabricated percentages.
