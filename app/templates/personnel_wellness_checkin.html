Voluntary Personnel Wellness

Wellness Check-In

This voluntary check-in helps VeerPulse understand changes in your normal wellbeing pattern. Your responses are considered together with duty, rest, leave and workload information. VeerPulse does not use this questionnaire to make a medical diagnosis.
Wellness Questionnaire 5 Sections
SECTION 01

Work & Duty Pressure

Think about your recent duty experience.

My current workload feels difficult to manage.
I have had enough recovery time between recent duties.
Recent changes in duty or deployment have been difficult to adjust to.
SECTION 02

Rest & Energy

Consider your recent sleep, rest and physical recovery.

My recent sleep and rest quality has been good.
My energy level has been lower than usual.
SECTION 03

Family & Social Connection

Consider your recent connection with family or trusted people.

I have been able to stay connected with family or people important to me.
Being away from family has been difficult for me recently.
SECTION 04

General Wellbeing

Compare how you feel now with your usual pattern.

I have been able to stay calm and focused during my normal activities.
I have felt more mentally tired or irritable than usual.
I feel noticeably different from my usual wellbeing pattern.
SECTION 05

Welfare Support

You can voluntarily request confidential welfare support.

Would you like confidential welfare support?
