Privacy-First Welfare Support

How VeerPulse Uses Your Data

VeerPulse is designed to support personnel welfare using minimum necessary information, controlled access and explainable indicators. The following principles describe the intended use of the system.

Minimum Necessary Data

Only information needed for welfare-support functions should be collected and displayed.

Role-Based Access

Personnel access is intended for their own records. Officer access should be limited to authorised welfare review.

Non-Disciplinary Use

Welfare indicators support human review, rather than automatic disciplinary decisions.

Explainable Indicators

Welfare attention should be linked to recorded evidence and understandable contributing changes.

No Medical Diagnosis

Wellness indicators and experimental camera measurements are not medical diagnoses.
