About VeerPulse

Early welfare support through explainable insights

Personnel serving in uniformed forces often work under demanding schedules, extended deployments, irregular duty hours and prolonged separation from family. VeerPulse focuses on identifying changes from an individual's normal pattern rather than making medical diagnoses.
