Privacy Principles

VeerPulse is designed around minimum-necessary access, confidentiality and human welfare support.

Minimum Necessary Data Welfare officers should only access information required for legitimate support and case review.
Role-Based Access Personnel and Welfare Officer portals have separate permissions and protected routes.
Voluntary Wellness Inputs Optional check-ins and camera-derived physiological estimates should be used only with consent and as supporting context.
Human Review Required Welfare alerts must be reviewed by authorized personnel before any support action is considered.

Responsible Use

Welfare information must not be treated as a medical diagnosis or automatic disciplinary evidence.

Non-Disciplinary Purpose Welfare signals are intended for support, workload awareness and timely human follow-up.
Explainable Alerts Officers should be able to understand which pattern changes contributed to a welfare alert.
No Medical Diagnosis VeerPulse does not diagnose stress disorders, mental illness or other medical conditions.
Confidential Handling Sensitive information should only be shared with authorized personnel where necessary.
