Optional Camera-Based Wellness Measurement

Wellness Scan

Use your camera for a short voluntary wellness measurement. This experimental 45-second scan estimates pulse and, when supported by a repeatable signal, breathing rate. Video is processed in this browser; only usable results are sent when you choose Save. Please sit still, face the camera directly and ensure that your face is clearly illuminated.

Measurement Flow

How the Wellness Scan Works

1
Camera Capture

The user voluntarily positions their face in front of the camera.

2
Signal Analysis

Facial video may be processed using camera-based physiological signal technology.

3
Wellness Indicators

Supported physiological indicators are calculated by the measurement engine.

4
Trend Comparison

Valid measurements may be compared against the person's own historical baseline.

Optional Additional Data

Wearable Wellness Information

Sleep History Optional Wearable Source

Sleep duration and recovery history cannot be determined from a short face scan and may later be obtained from an authorized wearable device.

Activity History Optional Wearable Source

Steps, activity and longer-term movement trends may later be imported with user consent.

Important: Wellness Scan is intended for wellness trend support, not medical diagnosis. Experimental camera-derived wellness measurement. Not for medical use. Pulse and breathing estimates depend on optical signal quality. This scan does not calculate HRV, blood pressure, SpO2, physiological stress or cardiac workload.
